- Built a feature-rich marketplace with customer storefront, seller dashboard, and admin dashboard.
- Implemented OTP-based authentication, JWT authorization, and role-based routing.
- Added product catalog, cart, wishlist, orders, coupons, reviews, transactions, and seller reporting modules.
- Integrated payment support with Razorpay/Stripe and an AI customer-support chatbot using Google Gemini.
- Used Redux Toolkit for frontend state management and MongoDB/Mongoose for backend data modeling.
